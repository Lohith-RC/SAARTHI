## 1. Environmental Thresholds for High-Value Indoor Crops

### A. Oyster Mushroom (*Pleurotus ostreatus*)
* **Spawn Run (Colonization):** Temp 24–28°C | RH 85–90% | $CO_2$ 5,000–10,000 ppm (High $CO_2$ promotes mycelium) | Light: 0 Lux (Complete Darkness).
* **Pinhead Formation:** Temp 18–22°C | RH 95–98% | $CO_2 < 900$ ppm (Triggered by fresh air drop) | Light: 500–1000 Lux.
* **Fruiting Body Growth:** Temp 20–24°C | RH 85–90% | $CO_2 < 850$ ppm | Cycle: 4–6 Days.

### B. Shiitake Mushroom (*Lentinula edodes*)
* **Fruiting Stage:** Temp 16–20°C | RH 85–90% | $CO_2 < 1,000$ ppm | Light: 800 Lux (12h photoperiod).

### C. Hydroponic Butterhead Lettuce (NFT / Deep Water Culture)
* **Water Chemistry:** pH 5.6 – 6.2 | EC 1.2 – 1.8 mS/cm | Water Temp 18–21°C | Dissolved Oxygen $> 6.5$ mg/L.
* **Ambient Air:** Temp 20–23°C (Day), 16–18°C (Night) | RH 60–70% | $CO_2$ 800–1,200 ppm.

## 2. Pathogen Diagnostics & Remediation Matrix

| Pathogen / Anomaly | Visual Symptoms | Environmental Cause | Saarthi Autonomous Remediation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Green Mold (*Trichoderma*)** | Patches of white mycelium rapidly turning dark green on substrate. | High humidity ($>95\%$) combined with stagnant air and poor air filtration. | 1. Flag chamber for immediate isolation.<br>2. Advise spot treatment with $3\%$ Hydrogen Peroxide or salt barrier.<br>3. Ramp exhaust ventilation. |
| **Long Stems / Small Caps (Stipe Elongation)** | Mushrooms developing long, rubbery stalks with tiny deformed caps. | Severe $CO_2$ suffocation ($>1,200$ ppm during fruiting). | 1. Auto-trigger continuous Fresh Air Exchange (FAE) fan for 15 mins.<br>2. Reduce damper recirculation. |
| **Tip Burn in Lettuce** | Browning/necrosis on inner leaf margins of hydroponic greens. | Calcium deficiency caused by low transpiration (excess humidity or stagnant air). | 1. Increase air velocity around plant canopy.<br>2. Adjust nutrient solution EC by $-0.2$ mS/cm. |
