## 1. Tough Investor Questions & Winning Answers

### Q1: "What if a big tech company like Google or Microsoft builds this?"
* **Our Answer:** *"Big tech builds horizontal platforms (like Microsoft FarmVibes), not specialized edge hardware and localized voice copilots for mushroom and hydroponic cleanrooms. They lack the ground-level distribution partnerships with local seed/spawn dealers that form our defensible distribution moat."*

### Q2: "Why wouldn't growers just buy a cheap $15 smart plug on Amazon?"
* **Our Answer:** *"A generic smart plug has no agronomy brain. It doesn't know the difference between high $CO_2$ during mushroom colonization (good) versus fruiting (fatal). Saarthi is not an on/off timer—it is an automated agronomist and crop insurance system."*

### Q3: "How do you scale from 50 urban rooms to rural smallholders?"
* **Our Answer:** *"Urban indoor growers give us immediate high-margin B2B cash flow today. We use that revenue to fund our Phase 3 offline edge models and localized dialect voice models for rural smallholders without depending on slow government subsidies."*
