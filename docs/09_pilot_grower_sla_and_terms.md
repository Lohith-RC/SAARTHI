## 2. Core Terms & Conditions

### A. Hardware & Advisory Disclaimer (Liability Shield)
* **Advisory Tool Classification:** SAARTHI provides real-time monitoring, autonomous telemetry alerts, and agricultural suggestions.
* **Manual Override Requirement:** The Grower acknowledges that physical backup breakers and manual ventilation switches must remain operational. SAARTHI and its founders are not liable for direct crop damage resulting from external grid power blackouts or mechanical pump failures.

### B. Camera Placement & Video Privacy
* **Crop-Only Targeting:** Cameras installed under this program must strictly face cultivation substrate racks, plant canopies, or hydroponic troughs.
* **Zero Audio/Visual Eavesdropping:** Ambient voice microphones process speech-to-intent locally and do not record or store continuous audio conversations.

### C. Data Ownership & AI Training Rights
* **Grower Rights:** The Grower owns 100% of their physical crop, harvest yield data, and commercial financial records.
* **Saarthi License:** The Grower grants SAARTHI a non-exclusive, anonymized license to use temperature, humidity, $CO_2$, and leaf imagery telemetry to train and improve diagnostic machine learning models.
