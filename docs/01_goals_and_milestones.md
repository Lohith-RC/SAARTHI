# 🎯 SAARTHI: Master Goals, Subgoals & Milestone Schedule
> **Document Version:** 1.0.0 | **Status:** Active Engineering Charter

---

## 1. Executive North Star Vision
**"To eliminate preventable crop failure across high-value urban and indoor agriculture by 2030, empowering every grower with an autonomous, voice-first AI navigator."**

---

## 2. Phased Goal Hierarchy

### 🏆 Phase 1: Urban & Darkroom MVP (Months 1 – 3)
* **Goal 1.1: Functional Sensor-to-HUD Live Loop**
  * Target: < 2 second latency from physical CO2/RH reading to glowing Digital Twin HUD update.
  * Budget: Hardware Bill of Materials < ₹1,500 ($18).
* **Goal 1.2: Context-Aware Voice Copilot ("Hey Saarthi")**
  * Ambient entry greeting, hands-free telemetry queries, and verbal harvest logging.
  * > 90% intent recognition accuracy in high-noise fan environments.
* **Goal 1.3: 5 Pilot Grow Room Deployments**
  * Deploy 3 commercial Oyster/Button mushroom chambers + 2 hydroponic leafy-green containers.
  * Achieve zero crop mortality from overnight climate spikes during the 60-day trial.

---

### 🚀 Phase 2: Commercial Scale & Supply Chain Integration (Months 4 – 9)
* **Goal 2.1: Consumables Auto-Reordering Engine**
  * Partner with 3 regional mushroom spawn and hydroponic nutrient distributors.
  * 5–8% affiliate commission loop automated via Saarthi procurement portal.
* **Goal 2.2: Multi-Chamber B2B Fleet Management**
  * Centralized dashboard for farm owners operating 5 to 50 rooms simultaneously.
* **Goal 2.3: Verified Quality QR Certification**
  * Issue cryptographic batch certificates for zero-pesticide, precision-grown crops.

---

### 🌍 Phase 3: Planetary Scale & Smallholder Democratization (Months 10+)
* **Goal 3.1: Ultra-Low-Cost Edge Inference on Budget Phones**
  * Fully offline quantized SLM running on $50 Android devices in rural areas.
* **Goal 3.2: Multi-Dialect Regional Support**
  * Hindi, Telugu, Marathi, Tamil, Swahili, Tagalog speech models fine-tuned for noisy field conditions.
* **Goal 3.3: Spatial Web-AR & Tabletop Hologram Portals**
  * Immersive 3D augmented reality visualization for farm investors and executive offices.
